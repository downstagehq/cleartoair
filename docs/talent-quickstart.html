Going live from your phone

Clear to Air · Talent
1

Open the link

Tap it, or scan the code on the desk's screen. Use Chrome or Safari.

2

Allow camera + mic

Say Allow to both. You should see yourself and the green meter move.

3

Wear headphones

No speaker: the show would hear itself back (echo).

4

Press CONNECT

Wait for the green CONNECTED · LIVE. That's it - leave the page open.

What the light means

Connected · LiveAll good. The desk has your picture and voice.
Reconnecting…Hang on - it fixes itself. You are usually still on air.
Opened elsewhereYour link was opened on another device. Use that one, or tell the desk.
Link closedThe desk ended it. Ask them for a new link.
OfflineNot connected. Press CONNECT.
Connecting…Just pressed. Give it a few seconds.

Before you go live

Plug inVideo eats battery
Screen onA locked screen stops the camera
Do Not DisturbNo calls mid-show
Good signalStay on this page; don't switch apps
Stuck? Press DISCONNECT, then CONNECT again. Still stuck - message the desk.
