## What the marks are

- **The name** "Clear to Air", in any capitalisation or spacing.
- **The lamp mark**: the tick with its dot, as drawn in `brand/mark.svg` and its
  state variants (`brand/mark-*.svg`).
- **The wordmark**: "clear to a✓r" in lower case, where the lamp mark stands in
  for the i of *air* (`brand/wordmark*.svg`).
- **The lamp's four colours**, when used on or as the mark. The lamp is the
  product's status signal and its colours are part of how people recognise it:

  | State | Colour |
  |---|---|
  | Clear | `#3DDC84` |
  | Warnings | `#FFB020` |
  | Faults | `#FF5A5A` |
  | Idle | `#1E242C` |

  Those colours on their own are not claimed. Using them for a status light in
  your own product is fine. Combining them with a tick-and-dot shape, or anything
  else that reads as our lamp, is not.

The same files also appear as generated copies, for example in `web/src/brand.ts`,
`tools/launcher/brand/` and the site's icons.

## You can do these without asking

- **Say what your thing works with.** "A pack for Clear to Air", "works with Clear
  to Air", "an adapter for Clear to Air". Use the plain name in text. Do not use
  the mark or wordmark as your own logo, and do not suggest we made or endorse it.
- **Pass on official builds unchanged.** You can redistribute an official release
  zip as we published it, with the name and mark intact.
- **Write and talk about it.** Articles, tutorials, reviews, videos, streams and
  talks can use the name, the logo and screenshots to refer to the project.
- **Link to us.** You can use the name or the mark as a link to the project.
- **Use it on your broadcast.** Clear to Air puts no logo on your overlays, and
  you do not need to credit it on air.

## Forks and modified versions

If you distribute a modified version, or run one as a hosted service for other
people:

1. Give it a different name that is not confusingly similar to "Clear to Air".
2. Remove the lamp mark and the wordmark, and replace them with your own: in
   `brand/`, `web/src/brand.ts`, `tools/launcher/brand/`, the tray icons, the
   browser tab icon and any site you ship.
3. Do not use the lamp's four-state colour scheme as your logo.

You may say, as a plain statement of fact, that your project is "based on Clear
to Air" or "a fork of Clear to Air".

Private changes that you run only for your own shows are not distribution. You do
not need to rename those.

## Things that need permission

- A product, company, domain name or social account that includes "Clear to Air"
  or something confusingly close to it.
- Changing the mark or the wordmark, or combining them with your own logo.
- Merchandise with the name or the mark.
- Anything that suggests Downstage made, sponsors or endorses your product or
  event.

Ask first. Most reasonable requests will be a yes.
